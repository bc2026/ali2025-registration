How can I register?

Voting is a fundamental right! Follow the steps below to register to vote in New Jersey.

What You Will Need to Register Online

  • Your Date of Birth
  • One of the following forms of identification:
    • A current and valid Driver’s License or a Non-driver Identification Card issued by the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission (MVC)
    • Your Social Security Number (SSN)
  • If using your SSN, you must have the ability to sign on-screen or upload your signature to complete the registration process.

How to Register Online

  1. Visit the New Jersey Online Voter Registration Website: Go to the New Jersey Division of Elections website.
  2. Select Your Preferred Language: Choose your language if necessary.
  3. Provide Your Information:
    • Enter your date of birth.
    • Choose your form of identification (Driver’s License/ID or SSN).
  4. Provide Your Signature: If using SSN, sign on-screen or upload a signature.
  5. Submit Your Registration: Review your information and submit your registration.

Additional Information

  • The application will time out after 5 minutes of inactivity, clearing all entered data.
  • If you cannot register online, you can download the voter registration application and mail it in.

Am I Already Registered?

To check if you are already registered, visit the Am I Registered? page on the New Jersey Division of Elections website.

Mailing a Paper Registration Form

If you prefer to register by mail, download and print the voter registration form from the New Jersey Division of Elections website. Once completed, mail the form to:

New Jersey Division of Elections
P.O. Box 304, Trenton, NJ 08625-0304

Important Deadlines

  • You must register at least 21 days before Election Day to vote in any upcoming elections.
  • If registering by mail, ensure your form is postmarked by the deadline.
